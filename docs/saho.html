Saho

Saho is a guide and a gathering place for people learning on their own, in Malayalam, English or a mix of both.

Any institution (a library, a college, a panchayat, a hospital) can run its own Saho, and every Saho is linked to the others. Learners keep their own record of what they learn, and take it with them.

Saho is in development, and a library pilot comes next. It will be released as open-source software.
