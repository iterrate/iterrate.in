How we work

We started Iterrate because of our belief in the power of learning and technology. These principles shape what we build.

  1. Federated, not centralised

    No single company, including us, should hold everyone’s learning in one place. Institutions run their own copies of Saho, called instances (we call each one a place), and those instances work together.

  2. Learners own their records

    What you learn and say belongs to you. Places see only what you allow, and you can take that back.

  3. Local first

    Malayalam, and a mix of Malayalam and English, come first, not as an afterthought. Saho should run on modest hardware, close to the people who use it.

  4. Open source

    The software will be open, so that anyone can inspect it, run it and improve it, and so that no one is tied to us.

  5. Control over your AI

    People and institutions shouldn’t depend on a single platform or model. Saho works with whichever AI model a place chooses, including ones it runs itself.

    AI helps people think and find their way; it doesn’t replace teachers, librarians or each other.

Why now

Keralam aims to be a knowledge society. AI is changing what knowledge work looks like, and we want people here to learn how to use these tools in their work and lives on their own terms, without handing that over to a few distant platforms.

How Iterrate is paid

The basic platform is free for learners. Institutions pay us for licences, setup, hosting and support, or run Saho themselves.

An institution can add its own courses or fees, and learners can pay for premium features beyond what their institution covers.

We aim to price fairly and put any surplus back into the work.
