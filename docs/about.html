About Iterrate

Iterrate (OPC) Private Limited is a small company in Thiruvananthapuram, incorporated on 8 November 2024 and recognised as a startup by DPIIT and Kerala Startup Mission.

Who we are

Iterrate was founded by Sangeeth, its director. It is a one-person company for now. You can read more about Sangeeth at sangeethvarma.com.

What we do

We build Saho, and we help institutions set it up, run it and look after it. Read how we work.

The name is i + terra + te: a person, and the earth they stand on. Together it reads iterate: try, reflect, try again.

The logo draws that loop, with a sky line over the top and an earth line underneath, and a green arrow that breaks out at the end. Each turn of learning carries you out into the world.
