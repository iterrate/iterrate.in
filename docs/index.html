Tools that belong to the places that use them.

Iterrate is a small company building Saho, an open, federated learning platform. Any institution (a library, a college, a panchayat, a hospital) can run its own Saho, and learners own what they learn.

Saho

A guide and a circle for people learning on their own, at any stage of life. For finding a direction, not cramming for one exam.

What Saho is

Federated by design

Every institution can run its own Saho. They connect to each other, and nobody owns the whole network.

Our principles

Free to choose

People and institutions shouldn’t depend on one platform or one AI model. Saho lets them choose, and run their own.

Why it matters

Communities should own the tools they learn with.

Keralam built its public libraries village by village, through people who wanted knowledge to be shared and run locally. We want the software of learning to work the same way: open source, run close to the people who use it, and answerable to them.
